FAQs

How do I check if a service is down?

Look at the status beside each service above. If everything is operational but your site won't open, clear your browser cache, try another network or device, and check your DNS settings. Still stuck? Contact support.

What do the labels mean?

Operational: our checks passed.
Partial outage: some checks for that service failed.
Major outage: all checks for that service failed.
Status unknown: we don't have a recent result.

Which services are checked?

DNS, email, web hosting, the control panel, the account manager and our website. Individual customer websites and virtual servers are not checked here.

What is the difference between maintenance and an outage?

Maintenance is planned work that we announce here. An outage is an unplanned problem.
